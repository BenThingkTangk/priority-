Split focus across all 9 products today

Trying to sell everything simultaneously with a benched flagship spreads credibility thin. Narrow the story, protect the brand.

The market is moving fast on AI regulation. The companies that sprint past compliance right now are going to get fined, sued, or blacklisted by carriers and enterprise procurement teams. Slowing down SalesOS for 30–60 days to get this right is not losing ground — it is building the moat that no competitor who's still sprinting can replicate once the regulatory hammer drops harder.
